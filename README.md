# Sharaf & Nour wedding invitation

Open `index.html` in a browser. Visitors first see the interactive envelope; after opening it, the Quranic verse appears before the existing invitation sections. The site includes local floral artwork and the supplied song at `assets/wedding-music.mp3`. Music starts when the visitor opens the invitation and can be paused or resumed with the music control. Web fonts load from Google Fonts when online.

## Before publishing

- Replace the `href="#"` on `#mapLink` in `index.html` with the exact Google Maps URL.
- The RSVP form currently gives an on-page confirmation only. Connect it to an RSVP service or backend before relying on it to collect guest responses.

The opening animation respects reduced-motion preferences and runs locally without a server. Wedding details shown are from the supplied brief; the verse is Quran 30:21.
